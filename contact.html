    FAQ

    Asked before the first call

    Do you sign audits?

    No. We prepare your evidence, fix the gaps and coordinate with the auditor. Where the rules need a signed audit from an approved (empanelled) firm, that firm signs it.

    Why start with a paid diagnostic?

    It turns a vague problem into a written plan and a fixed quote. If you decide not to go ahead, you keep the report.

    Can you work with our existing team and tools?

    Yes. Most work happens inside your current systems. We write down what we build so your team can run it.

    Does my data leave my systems?

    We agree where data is kept before work starts, sign a confidentiality agreement and give our staff access only to what the job needs.

    Do you work with companies outside India?

    Yes. We support firms abroad on AI and data work and on setting up teams in India.

    How is the work priced?

    By deliverable, monthly service or agreed outcome, never by open-ended hours. The quote comes after the diagnostic.
