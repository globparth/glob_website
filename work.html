What an engagement looks like

Four sample scenarios that show typical work. They are not case studies of named clients.

SAMPLE S-01
EXPORTER · DATA AND AI

Emissions data for EU buyers

Figures are spread across plant sheets and nobody owns them. We find every source, build one monthly pipeline with checks for missing values and produce the report a verifier expects.

THE RESULT

A repeatable quarterly report and a named owner inside the company.

SAMPLE S-02
CO-OPERATIVE BANK · COMPLIANCE

Ready for the RBI directions

A new directive arrives and the IT team is two people. We assess the bank against the directions, review each outside vendor, rehearse a six-hour incident report and rank the fixes.

THE RESULT

A prioritised plan and an evidence folder ready for the approved auditor.

SAMPLE S-03
DISTRIBUTOR · AI ASSISTANT

Support questions answered

Staff answer the same product questions all day. We build an assistant that answers from the company’s own documents, passes refunds to a person and is tested on real past questions.

THE RESULT

Routine questions handled, every escalation logged, accuracy reported monthly.

SAMPLE S-04
STARTUP · CTO-AS-A-SERVICE

Investor due diligence

A funding round needs a clear view of the technology and its risks. We review the architecture, code practices, security and costs, and write findings investors and founders can both read.

THE RESULT

A due-diligence report with a ranked list of fixes and a 90-day plan.
