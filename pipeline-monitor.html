HOW IT WORKS

Five stages in every pipeline

A pipeline is one regular flow of data from a source to a destination, such as a daily sales export feeding a dashboard.

1

Extract

Upload a CSV, Excel or JSON file, paste rows from a spreadsheet, or point it at a web address that serves a data feed.

2

Transform

Build the clean-up as a list of steps: keep and rename columns, filter, fix dates and numbers, calculate new columns, remove duplicates, group and total, or look up values from another table. Each step shows its effect on a live preview.

3

Check

The output is tested for missing or renamed columns, too few rows, a sudden drop in volume, blanks, duplicates and bad numbers. Bad rows are held back with a reason.

4

Load

Clean rows go out as a CSV, Excel or JSON file, or are sent to a web address such as an automation tool or your own system.

5

Monitor

Every run is recorded: rows in and out, time taken, pass or fail. Failures come with a likely cause, a suggested fix and an alert ready to send.

WHAT IT DOES

Built for the people who own the numbers

  • Three kinds of source: files, pasted data and web addresses
  • Fifteen kinds of transform step, with a live preview and no code
  • Checks learned from a known-good run, then adjusted by you
  • Clean rows delivered and bad rows held back in a separate file
  • Scheduled runs for web-address sources while the tool is open
  • A monitoring view with runs per day, pass rate, rows delivered and run time
  • Alerts written for you and sent from your own WhatsApp or email
  • A searchable audit trail of every run, step change and fix
  • Backup and restore of the whole workspace
