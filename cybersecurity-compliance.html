Questions about this service

Do you sign audits?

No. We prepare your evidence, fix the gaps and coordinate with the auditor. Where the rules need a signed audit from an approved (empanelled) firm, that firm signs it.

We are a small firm. Do the data-protection rules apply to us?

If you handle personal data of people in India, they are likely to apply. The assessment tells you which duties apply and which do not.
