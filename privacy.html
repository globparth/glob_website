Privacy notice

What personal data this website collects, why, and how to use your rights under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Who we are

This website is run by Globriddge International Private Limited, Office No 10, 11th Floor, The Platinum Tower, Old Mundhwa Road, Tukaram Nagar, Kharadi, Pune, Maharashtra 411014, India (“Globriddge”, “we”). Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) we are the data fiduciary, which means we decide why and how your personal data is used.

We collect nothing else. The site has no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. Fonts are served from this site, so no font company sees your visit. A temporary marker in your browser runs the animation between pages. It holds no personal data and is cleared when you close the tab.

Who receives your data

  • Our own staff who handle enquiries.
  • Service providers who work for us: our website host and the service that delivers the enquiry form to our inbox. They may process data on servers outside India, as the law permits.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to 12 months after our last contact with you, then delete them. If you become a client, your details are kept under the terms of our agreement with you.

Your rights

  • See a summary of the personal data we hold about you and what we do with it.
  • Correct or update data that is wrong or out of date.
  • Erase your data when it is no longer needed.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time. It is as easy as giving it: send one email.
  • Nominate another person to use these rights for you if you die or are unable to act.
  • Complain to us and get a reply.

To use any of these rights, email from the address you used on the form. Withdrawing consent does not affect what we did lawfully before you withdrew.

Complaints

Send your complaint to . We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

This site is for businesses. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, tell us and we will delete it.

Security

We limit access to enquiry data to the staff who need it and protect it with reasonable security safeguards. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use personal data, we will update this page and change the date below. Last updated: 13 November 2026.
