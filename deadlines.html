India compliance deadlines: DPDP, RBI and EU CBAM

Four dates that create work for Indian firms, with a live count to each one.

EU carbon border rules

The Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) is live. Exporters of covered goods such as steel and aluminium need verified emissions data for EU buyers.

10 Apr 2026

Co‑operative bank cyber rules

Reserve Bank of India (RBI) directions for urban co-operative banks: outsourcing contracts to comply, plus encryption, vulnerability testing and incident reports within six hours.

13 Nov 2026

Consent Managers begin

The first stage of the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Rules that firms handling personal data will notice.

13 May 2027

Full data-protection compliance

Notices, consent, security safeguards, breach reporting and rights requests must all work.
